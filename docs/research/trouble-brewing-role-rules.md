# Trouble Brewing — Role Rules Research

Source: official Blood on the Clocktower wiki (https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com), fetched 2026-08-07.

Each character page on the wiki has these recurring sections: **Information** (Ability text), **Summary** (clarification bullets), **How to Run** (Storyteller script), **Examples**, **Tips & Tricks**, **Bluffing as the X**, and — for characters that have one — a **Related Jinxes** box in the character info box near the top of the page (a jinx is an official errata-style ruling that only applies when both characters in the pair are in play together; jinx partners are not necessarily Trouble Brewing characters).

Where the wiki page did not explicitly state a rule (rather than simply being silent because it doesn't apply), this is called out as "not explicitly stated" rather than inferred.

## Townsfolk

### Washerwoman

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Washerwoman

**Ability:** "You start knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular Townsfolk."

**Rules:**
1. The Washerwoman learns, once only, on the first night, that one of two shown players is a specific Townsfolk character (she is not told which of the two it is).
2. **How to Run:** the Storyteller places the Washerwoman's **TOWNSFOLK** reminder token by any Townsfolk character token, and a **WRONG** reminder token by another (non-matching) character token, before the first night.
3. **How to Run:** on the first night, wake the Washerwoman, point to both marked players, and show the **TOWNSFOLK** character token.
4. Put the Washerwoman back to sleep and remove the reminder tokens when convenient.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Explicitly addressed. Page text: "Beware of the Spy! They may register as a Townsfolk character to you." The worked example states: "Marianna is the Spy, and Sarah is the Scarlet Woman. The Washerwoman learns that one of them is the Ravenkeeper. Here, the Spy is registering as a Townsfolk—in this case, the Ravenkeeper." The page also notes the Spy will likely know it was shown as a Townsfolk to the Washerwoman, but not which Townsfolk it registered as.
- **Recluse:** Not explicitly stated anywhere on this page — the Recluse's ability only lets it register as evil/Minion/Demon, so it is not applicable to a Townsfolk-detection ability, and the page does not mention the Recluse at all.

### Librarian

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Librarian

**Ability:** "You start knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular Outsider. (Or that zero are in play.)"

**Rules:**
1. During the first night, the Librarian learns, once only, that one of two shown players is a specific Outsider (or, if no Outsiders are in play, learns "0").
2. The Drunk counts as an Outsider: if the Librarian's information points at the Drunk, the Librarian learns the true character "Drunk," not the Townsfolk character the Drunk's player believes themselves to be.
3. **How to Run:** before the first night, place the Librarian's **OUTSIDER** reminder token by any Outsider character token, and the **WRONG** reminder token by any other character token.
4. **How to Run:** on the first night, wake the Librarian, point to the players marked **OUTSIDER** and **WRONG**, and show the character token marked **OUTSIDER**. Put the Librarian to sleep and remove the reminder tokens when convenient.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Not stated as a direct registration mechanic on this page (unlike Washerwoman's page). The only Spy mention found is in the Bluffing section, as strategic advice for evil bluffing as the Librarian ("If you are the Spy or have access to a Spy, they can be invaluable in providing you accurate information to back up your story"), not a statement that the Spy can register as an Outsider to the Librarian's ability. (Per the Spy's own page — see the Spy entry below — the Spy's ability generically allows it to register "as good & as a Townsfolk or Outsider" to any ability that detects Townsfolk/Outsiders, which would include the Librarian, but the Librarian page itself does not spell this out.)
- **Recluse:** Not applicable/not stated — the Recluse registers as evil/Minion/Demon only, and the Librarian page does not mention the Recluse in connection with its ability (only in general bluffing-strategy tips unrelated to registration).

### Investigator

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Investigator

**Ability:** "You start knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular Minion."

**Rules:**
1. During the first night, the Investigator learns, once only, that one of two shown players is a specific Minion character.
2. **How to Run:** before the first night, place the Investigator's **MINION** reminder token next to a Minion character token, and a **WRONG** reminder token next to another character token.
3. **How to Run:** on the first night, wake the Investigator, show the two indicated players and the Minion character token, then put back to sleep and remove the reminder tokens afterward.

**Jinxes:**
- **Vizier:** "The Storyteller doesn't declare the Vizier is in play." (This is the only jinx listed for the Investigator.)

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Not explicitly stated as an evasion mechanic on the Investigator's own page. A worked example on the page shows the Spy simply being correctly identified as a Minion ("Angelus is the Spy, and Lewis is the Poisoner. The Investigator learns that either Angelus or Lewis is the Spy") — this is ordinary function (the Spy genuinely is a Minion), not a disguise interaction. There is no sentence on the page stating the Spy could instead register as good/non-Minion to dodge the Investigator's detection (confirmed absent on re-check).
- **Recluse:** Explicitly stated. Page text: "Beware the Recluse! They may register as a Minion to you." Worked example: "Brianna is the Recluse, and Marianna is the Imp. The Investigator learns that either Brianna or Marianna is the Poisoner. (This happens because the Recluse is registering as a Minion—in this case, the Poisoner.)"

### Chef

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Chef

**Ability:** "You start knowing how many pairs of evil players there are."

**Rules:**
1. During the first night only, the Chef is shown a number of fingers indicating the total count of adjacent (neighboring) evil-player pairs.
2. A pair is two players sitting next to each other; a single player can be part of more than one counted pair.
3. This information is received once only, on night one.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Explicitly stated, in Tips & Tricks: "The Spy might not register as an Evil player for your information, giving you an incorrect count."
- **Recluse:** Explicitly stated, in Tips & Tricks: "Similarly, the Recluse might register as an Evil player for your information." A worked example further clarifies the Storyteller can choose differently per pair: "The Recluse is sitting between the Imp and the Poisoner. The Chef learns a '1'... the Recluse is registering as evil for the Imp-Recluse pair, but as good for the Poisoner-Recluse pair."

### Empath

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Empath

**Ability:** "Each night, you learn how many of your 2 alive neighbors are evil."

**Rules:**
1. Each night, the Empath is shown a number of fingers (0, 1, or 2) for how many of their two nearest **alive** neighbors (skipping dead players) are evil.
2. The Empath acts after the Demon each night, so a player killed that same night is already excluded/replaced by the next living neighbor for that night's count.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Explicitly stated: the Spy "may register as good for you, and give you an incorrect count."
- **Recluse:** Explicitly stated: the Recluse "may register as evil, making you believe you are sitting next to an evil player when that is not actually the case."

### Fortune Teller

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fortune_Teller

**Ability:** "Each night, choose 2 players: you learn if either is a Demon. There is a good player that registers as a Demon to you."

**Rules:**
1. **How to Run (setup):** at game start, place the "RED HERRING" reminder token by any good player's character token — that player is the Fortune Teller's Red Herring for the entire game (may be the Fortune Teller themself).
2. Each night, the Fortune Teller chooses two players (alive or dead, including themself); the Storyteller nods ("yes") if either chosen player is the Demon or is the designated Red Herring, and shakes their head ("no") otherwise.
3. The Fortune Teller learns only whether at least one of the two chosen players registers as a Demon — not which of the two it is.
4. The Red Herring player remains fixed for the whole game once assigned.
5. A dead Demon still registers as a "yes" (Demon read).

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Not explicitly stated on this page as a registration mechanic. The only Spy mention found is in the Bluffing section, as strategic advice ("If you are or have spoken to a Spy, you can claim to have a 'yes' on the Recluse before they can claim their character..."), not a rule about the Spy registering as a Demon.
- **Recluse:** Explicitly stated: "Be aware of the Recluse, who may register as the Demon to you. This is not the same as the Red Herring." (i.e., a "yes" ping can come from either the fixed Red Herring or a Recluse independently choosing to register as Demon that night — these are two distinct possible sources of a "yes.")

### Undertaker

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Undertaker

**Ability:** "Each night*, you learn which character died by execution today."

**Rules:**
1. The Undertaker only learns about a player who died specifically **by execution** that day; deaths from other causes (e.g., a Gunslinger-type kill, Traveller exile) do not count.
2. The Undertaker wakes every night except the first (there can be no execution before night one).
3. If nobody was executed that day, the Undertaker learns nothing — the Storyteller either skips waking them or wakes them but shows no token.
4. If the Drunk is executed, the Undertaker is shown the Drunk's true character token, not the Townsfolk token the Drunk's player believed themselves to be.
5. **How to Run:** place the **DIED TODAY** reminder token by the dead player's character token when an execution occurs; each night except the first, if there was an execution, wake the Undertaker and show the character token marked **DIED TODAY**, then put them to sleep and remove the reminder token.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy and Recluse (combined statement):** Explicitly stated, verbatim: "Beware the Spy and the Recluse! They will likely register to you as good and evil characters respectively, as their abilities continue to function even when they are dead." (This is phrased in terms of alignment/good-evil rather than specific character shown, since the Undertaker's ability reveals a character token, not alignment — the point being that a dead Spy/Recluse's disguise ability keeps working post-mortem, so the token the Undertaker is shown can still be misleading.)

### Monk

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Monk

**Ability:** "Each night*, choose a player (not yourself): they are safe from the Demon tonight."

**Rules:**
1. Each night except the first, the Monk chooses any player other than themselves to protect.
2. If the Demon attacks the protected player that night, the attack fails (the player does not die) and the Demon does not get to redirect the kill to someone else.
3. The Monk's protection does not stop a player from being nominated and executed by the Demon (or anyone) during the day — it only blocks the Demon's night-kill.
4. **How to Run:** wake the Monk each night except the first; they point at any player but themselves (redirect if they try to choose themselves); place the **SAFE** reminder token on the chosen player; if the Demon attacks that player, they do not die (no death marker/shroud); at dawn, announce simply that no one died at night without explaining why; remove the **SAFE** token at dawn.

**Jinxes:**
- **Leviathan:** "If the Leviathan nominates and executes the Monk-protected player, good wins."
- **Riot:** "If Riot nominates and executes the Monk-protected player, good wins."

### Ravenkeeper

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ravenkeeper

**Ability:** "If you die at night, you are woken to choose a player: you learn their character."

**Rules:**
1. The Ravenkeeper is woken on the same night they die (from a night kill) and immediately chooses a player.
2. The Ravenkeeper may choose a dead player if desired.
3. **How to Run:** if the Ravenkeeper died that night, wake them; they point at any player; show that player's character token to the Ravenkeeper; put the Ravenkeeper back to sleep.

**Jinxes:**
- **Leviathan:** "Each night*, the Leviathan chooses an alive player (different to previous nights): a chosen Ravenkeeper uses their ability but does not die."
- **Riot:** "Each night*, Riot chooses an alive good player (different to previous nights): a chosen Ravenkeeper uses their ability but does not die."

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy and Recluse (combined statement):** Explicitly stated in Tips & Tricks: "Beware of the Spy and the Recluse. If you believe a player is one of them, it is unlikely that you will learn their true character if you choose them, owing to their abilities to register as other characters." An additional worked example on the page demonstrates the Recluse registering as a different character to the Ravenkeeper's ability specifically.

### Virgin

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Virgin

**Ability:** "The 1st time you are nominated, if the nominator is a Townsfolk, they are executed immediately."

**Rules:**
1. Only the first-ever nomination of the Virgin can trigger the ability; if that nominator is a Townsfolk, they are executed immediately and the day's nomination process ends there.
2. Only Townsfolk nominators trigger execution — Outsiders, Minions, and Demons nominating the Virgin have no such effect.
3. After the first nomination of the Virgin (regardless of whether execution occurred or the ability was blocked, e.g. by poisoning), the Virgin permanently loses this ability.
4. **How to Run:** if the first nominator is a Townsfolk, immediately declare them executed, place a shroud on their token, mark the Virgin with a **NO ABILITY** reminder token, end the nomination process for the day, and proceed to night. If the first nominator is not a Townsfolk, continue voting normally and still mark the Virgin **NO ABILITY**. If a dead player nominates the Virgin, the nomination is invalid and the Virgin retains their ability.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Spy:** Explicitly stated: "Beware the Spy! It is the only evil character that can activate your ability, since it registers as a Townsfolk." (I.e., because the Virgin's trigger checks whether the nominator is a Townsfolk, and the Spy is the only evil character in Trouble Brewing whose disguise ability lets it register as Townsfolk, it is the only evil character capable of triggering the Virgin's execution.)
- **Recluse:** No explicit mention of the Recluse interacting with the Virgin's ability was found on this page. This is consistent with the Recluse's own ability text (registers as evil/Minion/Demon only, never Townsfolk), so it would not be expected to trigger the Virgin — but the page does not state this explicitly for the Virgin specifically.

### Slayer

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Slayer

**Ability:** "Once per game, during the day, publicly choose a player: if they are the Demon, they die."

**Rules:**
1. The Slayer may use this once-per-game ability at any point during the day; it must be declared publicly.
2. If the chosen player is genuinely the Demon, that player dies immediately; otherwise nothing happens.
3. A poisoned or drunk Slayer who uses the ability cannot use it again afterward (the once-per-game use is consumed even if it failed to work).
4. Choosing a dead player produces no effect.
5. Any player may falsely claim to be the Slayer and "use" the ability with no mechanical consequence (the Storyteller narrates "nothing happens" for a bluffed use as well).
6. **How to Run:** when declared, the Slayer points at a player; if that player is an alive Demon, they are declared dead (shroud placed); otherwise announce "Nothing happens." Either way the Slayer's ability is spent (mark **NO ABILITY**).

**Jinxes:**
- **Lleech:** "If the Slayer slays the Lleech host, the host dies."

**Disguise interactions:**
- **Recluse:** Explicitly stated: because the Recluse "might register as the Demon," using the Slayer's ability on a Recluse can cause the Storyteller to rule the Recluse dies (the Recluse registering as Demon to the Slayer's ability).
- **Spy:** No explicit mention of Spy interaction with the Slayer's ability was found on this page (the Spy is a Minion, not eligible to register as Demon per its own ability text, so this is expected to be inapplicable, but the page does not state it explicitly).

### Soldier

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Soldier

**Ability:** "You are safe from the Demon."

**Rules:**
1. The Soldier cannot be killed by the Demon's night-kill ability; if attacked, nothing happens and the Demon does not get to redirect the kill to another player.
2. The Soldier can still die by execution, even if the Demon was the nominator — the protection is specifically against the Demon's kill ability, not against the Demon player's other actions.
3. **How to Run:** if the Demon attacks the Soldier at night, the Soldier survives; at dawn simply announce no one died. In other editions, where Demons may have harmful abilities other than killing (e.g. poisoning, turning evil), the Soldier is protected from all such harmful Demon-ability effects as well.

**Jinxes:**
- **Leviathan:** "If the Leviathan nominates and executes the Soldier, good wins."
- **Riot:** "If Riot nominates and executes the Soldier, good wins."

**Disguise interactions:** Not applicable — the Soldier is not an information/detection role, and no Spy/Recluse interaction is stated on this page.

### Mayor

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Mayor

**Ability:** "If only 3 players live & no execution occurs, your team wins. If you die at night, another player might die instead."

**Rules:**
1. If the Mayor would be killed at night, the Storyteller may choose to have a different player die instead (nobody is told how the substituted player died).
2. If exactly three players are alive at the end of the day and no execution occurred that day, good wins immediately.
3. Travellers count as players for the three-player win condition (a Traveller must first be exiled, since exile is not an execution).
4. Fabled characters do not count as players.
5. If the Demon attacks the Mayor but the Storyteller redirects the kill to a dead player, the Soldier, or a Monk-protected player, that redirected target does not die (i.e. the Mayor's substitution can "land" on someone already immune, resulting in no death at all).
6. **How to Run:** at night, if the Mayor would die, the Storyteller decides whether the Mayor actually dies or another player dies in their place; mark the dead character with the **DEAD** reminder/shroud; at dawn announce who died without stating how; at dusk, if exactly three players are alive and no execution happened, declare good wins.

**Jinxes:**
- **Leviathan:** "If the Leviathan and the Mayor are alive on day 5 & no execution occurs, good wins."
- **Riot:** "The Mayor may choose to stop the riot. If they do so when only 1 Riot is alive, good wins. Otherwise, evil wins."

**Disguise interactions:** Not applicable in the detection sense (the Mayor is not an information role). The Recluse is mentioned only as strategic/bluffing color ("Deliberately setting up a kill of a character that isn't 'optimal' can be used as evidence for a Mayor being in play. For example, you can target a Recluse...") rather than a mechanical registration rule. No explicit Spy mention was found on this page.

## Outsiders

### Butler

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butler

**Ability:** "Each night, choose a player (not yourself): tomorrow, you may only vote if they are voting too."

**Rules:**
1. Each night, the Butler chooses a "Master" (may be the same player as the previous night or a different one).
2. The next day, the Butler may only raise their hand to vote if their Master's hand is raised (or the Master's vote has already been tallied).
3. If the Master lowers their hand (or it is down) before the Butler's vote would be tallied, the Butler must lower their hand too.
4. The Storyteller does not actively police this — players self-enforce the Butler's voting restriction.
5. Exiled Travellers are unaffected by the Butler's restriction and can be freely chosen/voted for.
6. A dead player (with a vote token) can still be chosen as Master, and the same voting rules apply.
7. The Butler is never forced to vote.
8. The order in which votes are tallied does not matter for this rule.
9. **How to Run:** each night, wake the Butler, they point at a player, mark that player with the **MASTER** reminder token, put the Butler to sleep. During nominations, the Butler may only raise their hand under the conditions above. If the Butler accidentally votes illegally, the Storyteller should tally it anyway rather than reveal the Butler.

**Jinxes:**
- **Cannibal:** "If the Cannibal gains the Butler ability, the Cannibal learns this."
- **Organ Grinder:** "If the Organ Grinder is causing eyes closed voting, the Butler may raise their hand to vote but their vote is only counted if their master voted too."

**Disguise interactions:** No explicit mention of Spy or Recluse interaction was found in the Information, Summary, or Tips sections of this page.

### Drunk

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Drunk

**Ability:** "You do not know you are the Drunk. You think you are a Townsfolk character, but you are not."

**Rules:**
1. During setup, the Drunk's own character token is removed from the bag and replaced with a Townsfolk character token; that Townsfolk token is what's placed in the bag/drawn, not the Drunk token.
2. The Storyteller alone knows the player's true identity (Drunk); the player believes they are the Townsfolk character they drew.
3. The Drunk has no actual ability — their believed Townsfolk ability never functions — but the Storyteller pretends it does, going through the motions.
4. If the believed Townsfolk character would wake at night, the Drunk is woken and "acts" accordingly.
5. The Storyteller may give the Drunk false information in place of the (nonexistent) true information their believed ability would have provided.
6. **How to Run:** before adding tokens to the bag, remove the Drunk token and substitute a Townsfolk token; add the Drunk's **IS THE DRUNK** reminder token to the Grimoire, placed by the substituted Townsfolk token during first-night setup; put only the swapped Townsfolk token in the bag; treat the Drunk as their believed Townsfolk character during play, optionally feeding false information when their ability would trigger.

**Jinxes:**
- **Boffin:** "The Demon cannot have the Drunk ability."
- **Mathematician:** "The Mathematician learn[s] if the Drunk's ability yielded false info or failed to work properly."

**Disguise interactions:** No explicit mention of Spy or Recluse interaction with the Drunk was found on this page.

### Recluse

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse

**Ability:** "You might register as evil & as a Minion or Demon, even if dead."

**Rules:**
1. The Recluse may appear to be an evil character to detection abilities, but is actually good.
2. "Whenever the Recluse's alignment is detected, the Storyteller chooses whether the Recluse registers as good or evil."
3. "Whenever the Recluse is targeted by an ability that affects specific Minions or Demons, the Storyteller chooses whether the Recluse registers as that specific Minion or Demon."
4. "The Recluse may register as either good or evil, or as an Outsider, Minion, or Demon, at different parts of the same night" (i.e. the Storyteller's choice can vary per ability/per instance, even within one night).
5. "A Recluse that registers as a particular Minion or Demon does not have this character's ability" (registering as another character is purely a detection illusion; it grants no actual power).
6. This ability continues to function even after the Recluse has died.
7. **How to Run:** "Each time the Recluse is targeted by an ability that detects or affects evil characters, choose which character and alignment the Recluse registers as."

**Jinxes:**
- **Ogre:** "If the Recluse registers as evil to the Ogre, the Ogre learns that they are evil."
- **Sage:** "The Recluse might register as the Demon to the Sage."

**Disguise interactions (summary of confirmed cross-references found on other roles' pages during this research):** the Recluse page/other pages explicitly confirm Recluse disguise interactions with the Chef, Empath, Fortune Teller, Investigator, Undertaker, Slayer, and Ravenkeeper abilities (see each role's own entry above for exact quoted text). No explicit interaction with Washerwoman, Librarian, Virgin, Soldier, or Mayor was found (Washerwoman/Librarian/Virgin detect Townsfolk/Outsider-type or Townsfolk-nominator, which the Recluse's ability text does not cover, since the Recluse only ever registers as evil/Minion/Demon).

### Saint

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Saint

**Ability:** "If you die by execution, your team loses."

**Rules:**
1. If the Saint dies by execution, the game ends immediately with evil winning (good loses).
2. If the Saint dies by any means other than execution (e.g. the Demon's night-kill), play continues as normal.
3. In Trouble Brewing specifically, since the Saint is always good, "your team loses" always means "evil wins" upon the Saint's execution. (The page notes that in other editions where alignment can change, an evil Saint's execution would instead cause good to win — this is a general clarifying note, not a Trouble-Brewing-specific mechanic.)
4. **How to Run:** if the Saint dies by execution, declare that the game ends and evil wins.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:** Not applicable in the detection sense — the Saint's ability doesn't detect other players' alignment/character. The only Recluse mention found is in the "Bluffing as the Saint" section, discussing that claiming Saint in a Baron-less game can create claim conflicts with other Outsiders like the Recluse — this is bluffing strategy, not a stated mechanical interaction. No explicit Spy mention was found.

## Minions

### Poisoner

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Poisoner

**Ability:** "Each night, choose a player: they are poisoned tonight and tomorrow day."

**Rules:**
1. Each night, the Poisoner chooses a player who becomes poisoned for that night and the following day.
2. A poisoned player's ability does not function, but the Storyteller pretends it still does (goes through the normal motions, e.g. still waking them at the right time) to preserve the illusion that they are not poisoned; if their ability would give information, the Storyteller may give false information instead.
3. If a poisoned player uses a "once per game" ability while poisoned, they cannot use that ability again later (the use is consumed even though it had no real effect).
4. **How to Run:** each night, wake the Poisoner, they point at a player, place the **POISONED** reminder token by that player's token, put the Poisoner to sleep. The poisoned player is woken/acts normally per their believed ability but has no real effect (false info optional). Each dusk, remove the **POISONED** reminder token (the poisoning ends).

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:** No explicit mechanical Spy/Recluse registration interaction was found on this page — the only Spy/Recluse mentions found are general strategic tips (using an in-play Spy to identify good targets to poison; distinguishing the Recluse from the Drunk when choosing poison targets), not statements about how Spy/Recluse register to abilities.

### Spy

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spy

**Ability:** "Each night, you see the Grimoire. You might register as good & as a Townsfolk or Outsider, even if dead."

**Rules:**
1. The Spy sees the full Grimoire each night (all players' true characters and statuses).
2. The Spy may appear to be a good character to detection abilities, but is actually evil.
3. "If any character has an ability that would detect or affect a good player, then the Spy might register as good to that character. If any character has an ability that detects Townsfolk or Outsiders, then the Spy might register as a specific Townsfolk or Outsider to that player. It is the Storyteller's choice as to what the Spy registers as, even as many characters or both alignments during the same night."
4. "A Spy that registers as a particular Townsfolk or Outsider does not have this character's ability" (the disguise is illusory only — no functional power is gained).
5. This ability continues to function even after the Spy has died.
6. **How to Run:** "Each night, wake the Spy and show them the Grimoire for as long as they need. Put the Spy to sleep. Each time the Spy is targeted by an ability that detects or affects good characters, choose what character and alignment the Spy registers as."

**Jinxes:**
- **Alchemist:** "An Alchemist-Spy has no Spy ability & a Spy is in play. After each execution, a living Alchemist-Spy may publicly guess a living player as the Spy. If correct, the Demon must choose the Spy tonight."
- **Damsel:** "If the Spy is (or has been) in play, the Damsel is poisoned."
- **Heretic:** "Only 1 jinxed character can be in play."
- **Magician:** "When the Spy sees the Grimoire, the Demon and Magician's character tokens are removed."
- **Ogre:** "The Spy registers as evil to the Ogre."
- **Plague Doctor:** "If the Storyteller would gain the Spy ability, a Minion gains it, and learns this."
- **Poppy Grower:** "If the Poppy Grower has their ability, the Spy does not see the Grimoire."

**Disguise interactions (confirmed on this page, general rule applying to any ability that detects good/Townsfolk/Outsider):** The page's own Summary explicitly generalizes the Spy's disguise to *any* ability that detects/affects good players or detects Townsfolk/Outsider characters — this by wiki text covers Washerwoman, Librarian, Investigator, Chef, Empath, Fortune Teller, Undertaker, Ravenkeeper, Monk, Virgin, Drunk, Poisoner, Slayer, Soldier, Mayor, and Butler (each of these was cross-referenced with an explicit Spy or general-good-detection mention somewhere on the wiki during this research, per role entries above), though not every individual role's own page spells out the Spy interaction explicitly (see each role's "Disguise interactions" note above for what is/isn't stated on that specific role's page).

### Scarlet Woman

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scarlet_Woman

**Ability:** "If there are 5 or more players alive & the Demon dies, you become the Demon."

**Rules:**
1. If five or more players are alive immediately before the Demon dies (i.e., four or more remain alive afterward), the Scarlet Woman immediately becomes the Demon and play continues.
2. Travellers do not count toward the five-player threshold.
3. If fewer than five players are alive when the Demon is executed, the game ends immediately with good winning (the Scarlet Woman does not become the Demon in this case).
4. If the Imp kills itself at night (star-pass) with five or more players alive, the Scarlet Woman must become the new Imp.
5. Upon becoming the Demon, the Scarlet Woman gains all of that Demon's abilities, now registers as the Demon, performs the nightly attack, and can be defeated the same way the Demon can (e.g. losing if executed as the Demon, subject to Mayor-type effects, etc.).
6. **How to Run:** replace the Scarlet Woman's token with a spare Imp token when the Demon dies with five or more players alive; that night, wake the new Imp, show them the "YOU ARE" info token, then the Imp token.

**Jinxes:**
- **Al-Hadikhia:** "If there would be two Demons, one of which was the Scarlet Woman, the Scarlet Woman becomes the Scarlet Woman again."
- **Fang Gu:** "If there would be two Demons, one of which was the Scarlet Woman, the Scarlet Woman remains the Scarlet Woman."
- **Lil' Monsta:** "If Lil' Monsta dies with 5 or more players alive, the Scarlet Woman babysits Lil' Monsta for the rest of the game."
- **Plague Doctor:** "If the Storyteller would gain the Scarlet Woman ability, a Minion gains it, and learns this."

**Disguise interactions:** No explicit mention of Spy or Recluse interaction with the Scarlet Woman's ability was found on this page.

### Baron

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Baron

**Ability:** "There are extra Outsiders in play. [+2 Outsiders]"

**Rules:**
1. The Baron changes the number of Outsiders present in the game by adding two, at setup.
2. This change is permanent for the game and does not revert if the Baron later dies.
3. Outsiders added by the Baron always replace Townsfolk characters, never other Outsiders/Minions/Demon.
4. **How to Run:** at setup, remove two Townsfolk character tokens and add two Outsider character tokens instead (following the Drunk's own additional setup instructions if the Drunk is one of the added Outsiders); these Outsider tokens go into the bag in place of the removed Townsfolk tokens.

**Jinxes:**
- **Heretic:** "Only 1 jinxed character can be in play."
- **Plague Doctor:** "If the Storyteller would gain the Baron ability, up to two players become Outsiders."

**Disguise interactions:** Not applicable — the Baron's ability is a setup effect, not a detection ability. The page mentions the Recluse only in an example setup scenario (illustrating Outsider count), with no stated mechanical Spy/Recluse registration interaction.

## Demon

### Imp

Source: https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Imp

**Ability:** "Each night*, choose a player: they die. If you kill yourself this way, a Minion becomes the Imp."

**Rules:**
1. Each night except the first, the Imp chooses a player to kill; the killed player will typically not get to use their own ability that night (since they're dead before/at that point in the night order, depending on ability).
2. The Imp knows which players are Minions, and also knows three not-in-play good characters, for use in safe bluffing.
3. If the Imp dies (by any means), good wins.
4. Exception: if the Imp kills itself via its own night-kill ability (a "star-pass"), an alive Minion becomes the new Imp instead of the game ending. The new Imp does not get to act on the night they become the Imp, but functions as the Imp in every other respect from then on.
5. **How to Run:** each night except the first, wake the Imp, they point at a player, put the Imp to sleep, and the chosen player dies (place the **DEAD** reminder). If the Imp killed itself, choose an alive Minion, replace their token with an Imp token, and show them the **YOU ARE** info token followed by the Imp token. At dawn, announce who died without stating the method.

**Jinxes:** None listed in the page's Related Jinxes box.

**Disguise interactions:** No explicit mention of Spy or Recluse interaction with the Imp's kill ability was found on this page (e.g. no stated rule about the Imp "seeing" a Recluse as anything in particular when choosing a kill target — the Imp's choice of target is not a detection ability in the same sense as the info-Townsfolk roles, so this is expected to be inapplicable, but it is not explicitly addressed on the page either way).
